The 7 Biggest Gaps Holding MetMic Back

These aren't opinions. They're observable gaps that your competitors are already exploiting. Each one is costing you borrowers, investors, or both.

01

No Social Proof Architecture

You've funded 73 mortgages totaling $80M+. You've delivered 8.81% annual returns for investors over 5 years. But none of this is on your website in a way that builds trust. Every competitor who adds real testimonials with names, numbers, and outcomes will outconvert you.

02

Your Team's Credibility Is Invisible

Suki Gill holds a CPA, CGA. Gurpinder Lally holds a CPA, CA. In financial services, these designations are massive trust signals. But your "Meet the Team" page doesn't showcase this. A borrower handing over a $1.5M mortgage decision needs to see WHO is behind the company.

03

No Content Moat

Most Canadians don't know what a Mortgage Investment Corporation is. The first company that educates the BC market with clear, simple content will own that conversation for years. Right now, MetMic has a blog section that's essentially empty. Cambridge MIC and PHL Financial are already producing content.

04

No Lead Generation Funnel

Your only conversion path is a contact form. No lead magnet for investors ("Free Guide: How MIC Investing Works"). No calculator tool for borrowers. No email capture. Every visitor who isn't ready to contact you today is lost forever.

05

Vague Speed Claims

Your positioning says "Speed & service over bureaucracy." But you never say HOW fast. "Funded in 5 business days" beats "we're fast" every time. Banks take 30-60 days. If MetMic can fund in 5-10, that's your headline. Not "speed."

06

Investment Funnel Has Friction

Investors click "Invest with Us" and get redirected to an external portal. Every redirect loses 30-40% of interested visitors. The investment process should feel like one continuous experience, not like you're sending them to a different company.

07

No Paid or Organic Acquisition

No Google Ads for "alternative mortgage lender BC." No Meta ads targeting accredited investors. No SEO content ranking for "MIC investing Canada" or "private mortgage BC." Your only lead source appears to be broker referrals and word-of-mouth. That's not scalable.
