# SNS Nutrition - Content Strategy

**Client:** Sharan Scali / Snatches & Samosas
**Date:** February 16, 2026

---

## Content Strategy Overview

**Goal:** Support the launch of SNS Nutrition program with consistent content that:
- Builds trust and authority
- Drives traffic to landing page
- Nurtures leads into buyers
- Engages existing community

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Myth Busting (40%)
Debunking diet myths that keep women stuck

**Angles:**
- "Why calorie counting doesn't work"
- "Why keto didn't work for you"
- "Why you're always hungry"
- "Why exercise isn't the answer"
- "The hormone problem no one talks about"

**Format:**
- Carousel posts (Instagram)
- Reels with voiceover
- Short YouTube videos

---

### Pillar 2: Transformations (30%)
Real results from real clients

**Angles:**
- Client spotlights (with permission)
- Before/after stories
- "What I learned" client quotes
- Results breakdowns

**Format:**
- Video testimonials (repurpose from YouTube)
- Carousel posts with quotes
- Story posts

---

### Pillar 3: Behind the Scenes (20%)
Sharan's personal journey and process

**Angles:**
- Why I don't do meal plans
- My own transformation
- What a typical week looks like
- How the method works
- Q&A from DMs

**Format:**
- Reels/videos
- Carousels
- Stories

---

### Pillar 4: Education (10%)
Teaching the method

**Angles:**
- How hormones affect weight
- Food neutrality explained
- Sustainable vs unsustainable approaches
- Why habits matter more than willpower

**Format:**
- YouTube content
- Carousel posts

## Key Metrics to Track

- **Reach:** How many see content
- **Engagement:** Likes, comments, shares
- **Click-through:** Profile visits, link clicks
- **Conversions:** Landing page visits, purchases
- **Cost per lead:** Ad spend / leads
- **Cost per purchase:** Ad spend / sales

---

## Content Production Workflow

1. **Plan:** Weekly content calendar (Sunday)
2. **Create:** Batch content (1-2 days/week)
3. **Post:** Schedule via Hypefury/Buffer
4. **Engage:** Reply to comments/DMs (daily)
5. **Repurpose:** Turn top performers into more content
6. **Analyze:** Weekly review of what worked
