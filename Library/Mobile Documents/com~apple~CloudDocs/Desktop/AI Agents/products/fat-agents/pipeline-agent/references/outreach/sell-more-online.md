### 11I. Value-Based Follow-Up

# Sell More Online - Value-Based Follow-Up

_Source: Sell More Online - Value-Based Follow-Up.docx_

## Value-Based Follow-Up
Module: Dream 100 Scaling
Group: Sell More Online
Source: https://www.skool.com/sellmoreonline/classroom/2264dd6a?md=718e5214a7b349eb8e8b48f577d13d67

The concept of Value-Based follow-up is very simple...
Every single piece of follow-up you do should have CLEAR value to the prospect.
When we follow up we are sharing:
- New tips & strategies
- Expansions on the original deliverable
- Something sick you did for another client today
- Something else you noticed that they can improve
- Relevant article or case study
- Creative follow ups
- Internal SOP that applies to them
- YouTube video that applies to them
It's all about providing genuine value in EVERY interaction with a potential client.
Make it personalized. Make it valuable. Put effort into it.

### Strategies To Increase Responses
### You can also implement Power Ups:
You want to make your Dream 100 deliverable stand out AND be easy for the prospect to implement.
Get creative. For example, if giving prospect free Ad Scripts, say 'I will literally get you an Uber Black, coffee, & coordinate a studio in your city to film these. All you have to do is show up and read off of a teleprompter for an hour'.
Think about what would be logical quick wins for your prospects.
You can add these verbiages/offers into the outreach scripts and/or deliverable documents.
