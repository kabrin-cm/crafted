# PART 1: D100 OVERVIEW AND ORIGIN

## What Is D100

### Origin

D100 is a lead generation tool originally coined by Russel Brunson.

- You create a list of 100 prospects that you would love to work with.
- Typically these are top of market prospects.
- Prospects that it would literally be a 'dream' to work with.

### How It Works

You do real upfront work for a prospect BEFORE reaching out to them. This is to highlight your competence and expertise. The more in depth and better you explain the solution to their problem the better.

**Note:** You need to be specific with D100. A lack of specificity will kill your D100 before you even start.

### Examples

1. **Content Agency** -- Script, edit, package and optimize one free YouTube video
2. **Lead Gen Agency** -- A full clay table with qualified leads
3. **Ecom Email Marketing Agency** -- A full email workflow with designs they can use

**Note:** All of the above examples are highly specific to the individual. When doing D100 the only person on planet earth that can benefit from that specific D100 should be your prospect.

### Results Within CA/AIAA

- Used D100 to sign Gym Launch
- Used D100 to run up to 80K+ per month
- Used D100 to sign Mark Tilbury (6M+ followers)
- Used D100 to sign Naam Wynn (550k+ followers)

## Why D100

### No Results or Social Proof?

1. **Book Calls** -- If you have no results or social proof you can utilise upfront work to get people on the phone.
2. **Competence = Calls** -- By highlighting your skill from the jump prospects will get on the phone with you even with little social proof.
3. **Show How You Can Help** -- Simply because you have highlighted that you can help their specific situation.

### Foot In The Door

1. **Performance Basis** -- If you have no social proof, you MUST work on a performance basis.
2. **Foot In The Door** -- D100 is a very easy way to get your foot in the door to work on a performance basis or get paid after the set of deliverables.
3. **Upfront Competence** -- Why? They can see that you are competent.

### Sign Whales

1. **Sign BIG CLIENTS** -- With D100 you can sign people that you frankly have no business signing.
2. **Stand Out** -- Whales get hit with garbage pitches constantly so if you do the upfront work then you can stand out and sign very large people.
3. **Best Method for Whales** -- These people would NOT have responded to your cold email.

## Pros & Cons

### Cons

1. Upfront Work
2. Need A Good Understanding of What Your Market Actually Wants (Can Take Time)
3. (Potential) Deliverability Issues

### Pros

1. Sign People With No Social Proof or Results
2. Sign Whales
3. Build Massive Trust
4. More Market Touch Points

# PART 2: PSYCHOLOGY OF WINNING D100 (7 Principles)

## Principle 1: Highlight Your Social Proof

### Social Proof

Highlight in granular detail people just like them who have benefited from your service. And say the EXACT result they got from it too.

### Show A Case Like Theirs

If you have no social proof you can use other successful cases to explain why it will work.

For example: I have never ran a quiz funnel. But Kai Lode (current student in CA) has run it successfully many times. I would take Kai's results and explain how I can do the same for them and the process I will follow.

**NOTE:** Do not pass off results of others as your own. D100 is often going to industry leaders, if they find out and tell others you will be isolated out of markets before you know it. Make it clear that these are the results of others. But you will follow similar process and work toward similar results.

## Principle 2: Specificity To Them

### Specific

Your D100 must be specific to the prospect, every single time. The only person on planet earth who should be able to benefit from that specific D100 is that prospect. Make it clear that it is for them specifically.

### Highlight Problems AND Why They Matter

Highlight the issues you see and why it is an issue worth solving. Prospects and human beings in general will typically lean toward inaction because it requires 0 effort. You must highlight why their current problem is a real problem. There is a 0% chance that someone will pay you unless they can see that this is an actual problem worth solving.

### Provide Real Solutions

Never ever tell the prospect what the solution is, GIVE them the solution. This means you want your prospect to execute no labour whatsoever to implement your D100 or benefit from it. We want to provide a complete end-to-end solution for them.

> "The more Done-for-you a service is, the easier it is to sell" - Daniel Fazio

## Principle 3: Highlight Real Problems

Highlight the issues you see and why it is an issue worth solving. Prospects and human beings in general will typically lean toward inaction because it requires 0 effort. You must highlight why their current problem is a real problem. There is a 0% chance that someone will pay you unless they can see that this is an actual problem worth solving.

## Principle 4: Dream State & Nightmare State Focus

### What REALLY Annoys Them

What are the things that your prospect wants to avoid at all cost. Highlight how your deliverable reduces that pain.

### What Does Your Prospect REALLY Want

What is the thing your prospect wants more than anything.

Example:
- Overweight person:
  - Their dream state is to lose 80lbs and look very fit
  - Their nightmare state is going to the pool and being shirtless

## Principle 5: Give FULL Solutions

Never ever tell the prospect what the solution is, GIVE them the solution. This means you want your prospect to execute no labour whatsoever to implement your D100 or benefit from it. We want to provide a complete end-to-end solution for them.

> "The more Done-for-you a service is, the easier it is to sell" - Daniel Fazio

## Principle 6: Tie Next Steps Into A Call or Contact

Once you have given them your solution make sure you tie next steps in. Prospects will often email back saying this is useful. But you then need to explain how you can:

- Implement it for them
- Create more for them like the deliverable provided
- Do even more work for them

Largely you must show the prospect that there is more coming for them if they want.

## Principle 7: Make It Easy For The Prospect

### Assume They Are Having A Bad Day

Assume your prospect is hungover, sick and his girlfriend has cheated on him. Make it as easy as humanly possible to consume your D100.

### Keep Things Legible

Do not use fancy fonts, themes or anything that makes it difficult to read or understand.

### Hemingway Grade 6

Make sure your D100 is below a grade 6 reading level. If your grandma cannot understand the sentence it is too complex.

Tool: https://hemingwayapp.com/

### Make Your D100 Dense

Exercise:
- If your mum had been kidnapped and you had to close a D100 prospect to get her back, how insane would you make your D100?
- What are all the deliverables you would do for someone if they paid you $10,000?

#### Pack Your D100

Your D100 needs to be filled with value for the prospect. It cannot be basic, they will not respond to something basic.

#### Common Objection

"This will take up too much time to do..." - D100 non believer

1. **Too Much Time At First** -- When you start D100 you will likely be: slow, produce a poor document, get little results. This is to be expected, once you understand how to create a good document none of the above will be real issues.
2. **Utilise AI** -- "I have not created a D100 gamma doc in 6 months. The lady who creates it for me can create a full D100 gamma doc in 7 minutes whilst watching Netflix if she wanted to. All AI."

### The Caribbean Analogy

If two people offered you:

1. A Free Stay At A Hotel In The Caribbean But You Had To:
   - Pay for travel costs
   - Pay for food
   - Pay for excursions
   - Sort out the logistics in your business
2. A Free Trip All Expenses Paid & Logistics Sorted To The Caribbean

What are you picking?

Make your D100 completely and utterly done-for-you. Done-for-you is premium. Done-for-you is a trip to the Caribbean all expenses paid.

# PART 3: D100 STRUCTURE (13-Step Template)

## How To Structure A Common D100

### Keys All D100 Needs -- The 13-Step D100 Structure

1. **Outcome-based headline** -- Focus on the result the prospect will get

# PART 5: THREE TYPES OF LOOMS (Complete Descriptions)

## Loom Theory

### Type 1: Looms At A Desk

You record a loom at your desk and go over your deliverable and how you can help. Basic screen recording.

**When to use:** Quick follow-ups, lower-priority prospects.

### Type 2: Cinematic Looms

You pre-record parts of the loom and then splice in bespoke parts for the prospect. For example: the hook, the reason for reaching out, etc.

**When to use:** Primary outreach (RECOMMENDED). This is the default for all D100 outreach.

### Type 3: Super Looms

You make them a bespoke video. Everything is original and the video is remarkable.

**When to use:** Top 20 whale prospects only.

### Loom Hierarchy

Looms At A Desk --> Cinematic Looms --> Super Looms

Further down you go the better the loom is (ceteris paribus).

### Why Loom Types Matter

In every D100 there are two levers that you are pulling:

1. **Attention** -- Getting the prospect to stop and notice you
2. **Expertise** -- Demonstrating your competence

There are instances where you need to focus on getting the prospect's attention first and foremost and then focus on the expertise after. With Cinematic Looms, we are leading with: **Attention.**

#### When Does Cinematic Work Best?

- You are TIRED of recording multiple looms at your desk
- Prospects actively do not care about your looms or watch them
- You want to stand out

#### Why Attention First?

Prospects are not watching a low quality camcorder loom. They have seen hundreds of them. You need to break the pattern.

#### Benefits of Cinematic Looms

1. Record Less & Get More Outputs
2. Stand Out from every other outreach they receive

# PART 6: CINEMATIC LOOM 5-PART STRUCTURE (Complete)

## The 5 Components

### Component 1: Hook (BESPOKE -- unique per prospect)

Do whatever you want with your hook but make it specific to the prospect. Seriously. The more creative the better!

- Their name
- Their business
- Something you noticed
- Something creative and unexpected

### Component 2: Reason For Reaching Out (BESPOKE -- unique per prospect)

Tell the prospect why you specifically reached out to them.

**CRITICAL NOTE:** You need to have this in. Tested removing this and results tanked. Prospects need to hear why you are reaching out to them specifically.

Reference:
- Their content
- Their growth
- A specific gap you noticed
- Something they said publicly

### Component 3: Value Shot 1 (PRE-RECORDED -- reuse across prospects)

Construction methodology:
1. **Identify the most common issue you see in your industry**
2. **Explain why it is an issue** -- Why the problem matters. People who have solved this problem.
3. **Explain how you solve it** -- "We can do solution 1"
4. **Explain why you are the person to solve it** -- Social proof. Show you understand their situation.

### Component 4: Value Shot 2 (PRE-RECORDED -- reuse across prospects)

Same construction as Value Shot 1 but for the SECOND most common issue:
1. **Identify the 2nd most common issue in your industry**
2. **Explain why it is an issue**
3. **Explain how you solve it** -- "We can do solution 2"
4. **Explain why you are the person to solve it**

**RULE:** Maximum of 2 value shots. No more.

### Component 5: CTA (PRE-RECORDED -- reuse across prospects)

**YOU MUST HIGHLIGHT THEIR DREAM STATE.**

#### Dream State Is Everything

Examples:
- Help you get 3 dates this month
- Help you lose 10lbs
- Help you go from 100K to 500K per month (Evan Seech example)

#### Bold CTA

You have done all of the hard work already, make sure you are bold with your CTA.

Examples:
- "Send me your login details, let's do this!"
- "I have your calendar I will book myself in"
- "Let me know when you can talk, we got work to do!"

## Value Shot Construction Methodology (Detailed)

For each Value Shot, follow this exact 4-step process:

**Step 1:** Identify the most common issues you see in your industry (pick top 2 across both Value Shots)

**Step 2:** Explain why they are issues
- Why does this problem matter to the prospect's bottom line?
- Who are people/companies who have solved this problem?

**Step 3:** Explain how you solve them
- Concrete solution 1
- Concrete solution 2

**Step 4:** Explain why you are the person to solve them
- Social proof (specific results)
- Demonstrate you understand their exact situation

## Production Efficiency

- Pre-record Value Shots 1, 2, and CTA once (these repeat across all prospects)
- Record unique Hook + Reason for each prospect (2-3 min each)
- Result: **50+ Cinematic Looms per day**
- Use AI tools (Gamma, Tome) to generate deliverable docs in ~7 minutes each

## Key Points Summary

1. Pre-record parts which are very similar across prospects
2. Pre-record parts for specific niches (optional -- if you serve multiple niches)
3. Always tell the prospect why you reached out

# PART 7: D100 FUNNEL FLOW & BOTTLENECK DIAGNOSIS FRAMEWORK

**Note:** This assumes your D100 lead list is strong and people actually want your offer. Not accounting for a poor offer, a poor lead list and other factors. Ceteris paribus.

## The D100 Funnel Flow

```
Not Opened D100 --> Opened D100 --> Booked Call --> Prospect Shows --> Close Deal
```

Each stage has a single goal:

| Stage | Goal |
|-------|------|
| Not Opened D100 | Get them to OPEN the D100 |
| Opened D100 | Get a response or call |
| Book Call | Get them to show up to the call |
| Close Call | Close the deal |

## Bottleneck Diagnosis (Stage by Stage)

### Stage 1: Not Opened D100

**If stuck here, fix:**
1. **Outreach Scripts** -- Your email is not compelling enough to get the click
2. **Relevant Stakeholders** -- You are sending to the wrong people

### Stage 2: Opened D100 But No Response

**If stuck here, fix:**
1. **D100 Strength** -- Your deliverable is not impressive enough. Upgrade quality, add more custom elements.
2. **Follow-Up Strength** -- Your follow-up emails are weak or non-existent

### Stage 3: Responding But No Booked Calls

**If stuck here, fix:**
1. **Booked Call Emails** -- Your call-booking process has too much friction. Simplify to one-click Calendly, offer alternatives.

### Stage 4: Prospect Does Not Show

**If stuck here, fix:**
1. **Sales Asset Strength** -- Not enough pre-call nurture. Add value-based follow-up between booking and call.

### Stage 5: Close Deal

**If stuck here, fix:**
1. **Sales Strength** -- Review call recording, adjust offer positioning. Sales execution or offer mismatch.

# PART 9: D100 BUYER JOURNEY & 3-STEP OPTIMIZATION

## Understanding D100 Buyer Journey

```
Create D100 --> Never seen D100 --> Send D100 --> Never seen D100 --> Opened D100 --> Seen D100
```

**Never Seen D100 Keys:** OPEN D100
**Seen D100 Keys:** Response / Call

## The 3-Step Optimization Framework

### Step 1: Optimize For The Click

What are we optimizing for firstly... THE CLICK.

1. **This Is Not M.A.C.E (Mass Automated Cold Email)** -- They are playing a different game to us. We are trying to get each individual person to open our email and doing EVERYTHING we can to have them open it. So let's do things differently.
2. **Your D100 Does The Selling** -- Can we conclude that if your D100 is specific, highlights their problems, the solutions to their problems and why you can solve them, your D100 will do the best job of selling your services than any email could?
3. **Get Them To Open It** -- So if your D100 is doing the selling for you, you need to optimize for them to open your D100 at all costs. That is your no.1 priority.
4. **So We Optimize For The Click** -- We are optimizing for them to open the D100 first and then we change what we are optimizing for after. You are far more likely to say yes to going on a date if you have a fun conversation first...

### Step 2: Optimize For A Response

After they open the D100, shift to optimizing for a response. You have two paths (see Offer vs. Value Follow-Up Decision Framework below).

### Step 3: Optimize For A Call

Once you get any form of response outside of "go away", you MUST optimize for a call.

# PART 10: CLICK OPTIMIZATION EMAIL PRINCIPLES

## How To Write Emails That Optimize For Clicks

### Principle 1: Pattern Interrupts

Think what your prospect has been through. Hundreds of thousands of rubbish pitches. Make the prospect stop and think. The last thing we want to do is be grouped in the bucket of terrible pitches just like everyone else.

### Principle 2: Separate Yourself From The Pack

Your D100 is highly unique. You are doing something that literally NO ONE else is doing. So you need to make that very clear in your email as well. Imagine you have done all this hard work and they don't even realise that you are putting in 10X the effort to everyone else. Total shame.

### Principle 3: Make The Prospect Think

If the prospect thinks the pitch is like everyone else, they will not continue to read. If the prospect has to ACTUALLY read the email to decipher whether it is the same then they will keep reading and get deeper in.

### Analysis Framework for Click-Optimized Emails

When writing or reviewing any D100 email, check these 4 elements:

1. **Click Optimization** -- There is no mention of a call, a response. All you are doing is getting them to OPEN the D100.
2. **Hard Pattern Interrupts** -- They have never in their lives got an email like this and they have never got a D100 like this in their life. You are different.
3. **Link At The End** -- The last thing they see is the link. They naturally want to click it. They are curious.
4. **Conviction** -- The prospect can feel you genuinely believe in what you created. Example: "We put in a lot (and I mean a lot) of effort to make it worth your time. At best: you'll think this is awesome! At worst: you'll appreciate the effort & move on (lol)"

## Email Checklist -- Click Optimization

### Copy
- [ ] Have you done a hard pattern interrupt?
- [ ] Is this the email you would send if you HAD to get them to open it?
- [ ] Is the email still professional whilst doing a hard pattern interrupt?
- [ ] Can the prospect feel your conviction?
- [ ] Are your sentences snappy?
- [ ] Have you used an opener that is not generic?
- [ ] Have you NOT opened with their name?

### Tech
- [ ] Hemingway Grade 6?
- [ ] Is your only CTA the click?
- [ ] Is it less than 100 words?
- [ ] Is the last thing they see a link?

### Avoided M.A.C.E
- [ ] Have you avoided M.A.C.E cliches? ("quick question" for example)

Optimize for the click as well as a booked call and close rate.

For example: If I said in an email, "open this or I will kidnap your mum" it will get a lot of opens but people won't take me serious enough to hop on a call.

# PART 11: OFFER VS. VALUE FOLLOW-UP DECISION FRAMEWORK

## After They Have Opened Your D100 You Have 2 Options

**CRITICAL RULE: WHEN SOMEONE OPENS YOUR GAMMA DOC MESSAGE THEM IMMEDIATELY. EVEN SEND THEM A PERSONALIZED VIDEO.**

### Option 1: Offer Follow-Up

If your D100 is great enough, you can just remind them of your offer and book calls.

### Option 2: Value Follow-Up

You can keep sending them more value to highlight your expertise.

### How Do You Choose Which One To Do?

Decide based on:
1. **Time** -- How much time do you have to invest?
2. **Capital** -- Do you have resources to create more deliverables?
3. **How Much You Want To Work With The Prospect** -- Is this a whale worth extra effort?

# PART 13: FOLLOW-UP EMAIL TECH (Style 1 & Style 2)

## Before Opening D100: Two Styles

### Style 1: Same Thread Forever (One Thread)

You keep all the follow ups in the same thread.

**Why this works:** The prospect can see the effort and read all of the emails at once, when they finally do pay you attention.

Data from: Penn & Felix Frank

#### Style 1 Thread Setup

```
Email 1 --> Email 2 --> Email 3 (same subject line)
Email 4 --> Email 5 --> Email 6 (same subject line)
Email 7 --> Email 8 --> Email 9 (same subject line)
```

### Style 2: New Thread Per Pod (3-Email Pods)

You do email threads of 3 emails at a time, then start a new thread with a new subject line.

**Why this works:** Eric Nowoslawski found that he would get less responses with each concurrent email. By starting a new thread with a new subject line etc, you reset your odds.

Data from: Eric Nowoslawski

#### Style 2 Thread Setup

```
Email 1 --> Email 2 --> Email 3 --> Email 4 --> Email 5 --> Email 6 --> Email 7 --> Email 8 --> Email 9 --> Email 10 --> Email 11 --> Email 12 (same subject line)
```

## After Opening D100: One Thread Only

Once they have opened, use ONE THREAD only. You keep all the follow ups in the same thread.

**Why:** We did all the hard work of getting them to open it, so let's remind them of what we did.

### After-Open Follow-Up Example

# PART 16: MULTI-STAKEHOLDER OUTREACH FRAMEWORK

## Core Rule

Send it to as many people as you can WITHOUT diminishing returns.

**Diminishing returns** = The point at which adding more stakeholders to your Dream 100 outreach list no longer produces proportional gains in replies, meetings, or deals because attention and relevance get diluted as volume increases.

## Correct Approach

D100 for CEO that has 100 employees. Send D100 to:
- **CEO** (primary decision maker)
- **CEO's Assistant**
- **Head of relevant department**
- **3-5 relevant employees in the department**

Result: Easy to forward your D100 to relevant person.

## Incorrect Approach

D100 for CEO that has 100 employees. Send D100 to:
- CEO
- 50+ irrelevant employees

Result: Not easy to forward your D100 and impossible to follow-up.
