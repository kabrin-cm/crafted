## Economics Formulas

**The Golden Rule:** Client LTV must be at least 5x their CAC, ideally 10x.

**The $15,000 LTV Rule:** For high ticket businesses selling through sales calls or sales meetings, CAC typically ranges from $1,500-$4,500. Therefore, your clients need an LTV of at least $15,000 for the economics to work.

**Product Prices:** This also typically means their products/services for sale should be between $5,000 - $10,000+

### Examples

- $3,000 CAC on a $15,000 LTV is 5x. This is doable. You can scale this business.
- $2,000 CAC on a $15,000 LTV is 7.5x. You can scale this business.
- $3,000 CAC on a $8,000 LTV is 2.6x. There's extremely little wiggle room here to make money. Not enough profit is being made to account for all additional business expenses.

5x LTV is normally the MINIMUM because you need to account for gross margins, which then gets into analyzing LTGP instead.

## Niche Evaluation Checklist

When evaluating a niche for viability, check:

1. **LTV at least $15,000** -- Can the client generate $15K+ in lifetime value from their customers?
2. **Product/service price $5,000-$10,000+** -- Are they selling high-ticket?
3. **CAC ratio 5x minimum** -- Is LTV at least 5x the cost to acquire a customer?
4. **Sells through calls or meetings** -- High-ticket B2B or B2C with a sales process
5. **Active demand** -- Are people actively searching for this solution?
6. **Willingness to pay for marketing** -- Do businesses in this niche already spend on marketing/lead gen?

### Red Flags (Niche to Avoid)

- LTV under $5,000
- Product prices under $1,000
- No existing ad spend in the market
- Commodity services with no differentiation
- Markets with zero online presence
