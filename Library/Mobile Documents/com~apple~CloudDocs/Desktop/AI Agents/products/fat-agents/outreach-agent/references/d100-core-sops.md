## What Is D100
### Origin
D100 is a lead generation tool originally coined by Russel Brunson.
- You create a list of 100 prospects that you would love to work with.
- Typically these are top of market prospects.
- Prospects that it would literally be a 'dream' to work with.
### How It Works
You do real upfront work for a prospect BEFORE reaching out to them.
This is to highlight your competence & expertise.
The more in depth and better you explain the solution to their problem the better.
Note: You need to be specific with D100. A lack of specificity will kill your D100 before you even start
### Examples
1. Content Agency — Script, edit, package and optimize one free YouTube video
2. Lead Gen Agency — A full clay table with qualified leads
3. Ecom Email Marketing Agency — A full email workflow with designs they can use
Note: All of the above examples are highly specific to the individual. When doing D100 the only person on planet earth that can benefit from that specific D100 should be your prospect.

## Why D100
### No Results or Social Proof?
1. Book Calls — If you have no results or social proof you can utilise upfront work to get people on the phone.
2. Competence = Calls — By highlighting your skill from the jump prospects will get on the phone with you even with little social proof.
3. Show How You Can Help — Simply because you have highlighted that you can help their specific situation.
### Foot In The Door
1. Performance Basis — In the video above Fazio says, if you have no social proof, you MUST work on a performance basis.
2. Foot In The Door — D100 is a very easy way to get your foot in the door to work on a performance basis or get paid after the set of deliverables.
3. Upfront Competence — Why? They can see that you are competent
Sidebar: Go watch the video above it is great for D100 and lead gen thinking.
### Sign Whales
1. Sign BIG CLIENTS — With D100 you can sign people that you frankly have no business signing
2. Stand Out — Whales get hit with garbage pitches constantly so if you do the upfront work then you can stand out and sign very large people
3. Best Method for Whales — These people would NOT have responded to your cold email

## Psychology of Winning D100
### Highlight Your Social Proof Or Social Proof
#### Social Proof
Highlight in granular detail people just like them who have benefited from your service.
And say the EXACT result they got from it too.
#### Show A Case Like Theirs
If you have no social proof you can use other successful cases to explain why it will work.
For example I have never ran a quiz funnel.
But Kai Lode (current student in CA) has run it successfully many times.
I would take Kai's results and explain how I can do the same for them and the process I will follow.
NOTE: Do not pass off results of others as your own.
D100 is often going to industry leaders, if they find out and tell others you will be isolated out of markets before you know it.
Make it clear that these are the results of others. But you will follow similar process and work toward similar results.
### Specificity To Them
#### Specific
Your D100 must be specific to the prospect, every single time.
The only person on planet earth who should be able to benefit from that specific D100 is that prospect.
Make it clear that it is for them specifically.
#### Highlight Problems AND Why They Matter
Highlight the issues you see and why it is an issue worth solving.
Prospects and human beings in general will typically lean toward inaction because it requires 0 effort.
You must highlight why their current problem is a real problem.
There is a 0% chance that someone will pay you unless they can see that this is an actual problem worth solving.
#### Provide Real Solutions
Never ever tell the prospect what the solution is, GIVE them the solution.
This means you want your prospect to execute no labour whatsoever to implement your D100 or benefit from it.
We want to provide a complete end-to-end solution for them.
'The more Done-for-you a service is, the easier it is to sell' - Daniel Fazio

### Tie Next Steps Into A Call or Contact
#### Provide Real Solutions And Tie In…
Once you have given them your solution make sure you tie next steps in.
Prospects will often email back saying this is useful.
But you then need to explain how you can:
- Implement it for them
- Create more for them like the deliverable provided
- Do even more work for them
Largely you must show the prospect that there is more coming for them if they want.
### Make It Easy For The Prospect
#### Assume They Are Having A Bad Day
Assume your prospect is hungover, sick and his girlfriend has cheated on him.
Make it as easy as humanly possible to consume your D100.
#### Keep Things Legible
Do not use fancy fonts, themes or anything that makes it difficult to read or understand
#### Hemmingway Grade 6
Make sure your D100 is below a grade 6 reading level.
If your grandma cannot understand the sentence it is too complex.
https://hemingwayapp.com/

## D100 Stakeholders
### Send It To As Many People As You Can Without Diminishing Returns
Diminishing returns = The point at which adding more stakeholders to your Dream 100 outreach list no longer produces proportional gains in replies, meetings, or deals because attention & relevance, get diluted as volume increases.
### Example
#### Correct
D100 for CEO that has 100 employees
Send D100 to:
- CEO
- CEO's Assistant
- Head of relevant department
- 3-5 relevant employees in the department

Easy to forward your D100 to relevant person
#### Incorrect
D100 for CEO that has 100 employees
Send D100 to:
- CEO
- 50+ irrelevant employees

Not easy to forward your D100 & impossible to follow-up
