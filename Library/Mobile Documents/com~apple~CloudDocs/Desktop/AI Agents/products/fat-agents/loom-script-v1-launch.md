### PART 6: The Commands Cheat Sheet (5:00 - 5:30)

**[Screen: Show the commands or a simple graphic]**

"Quick cheat sheet. You have 6 commands:

- `/pipeline` does everything. Research plus deliverables. This is the one most of you will use.
- `/research` if you just want the market intel.
- `/fulfillment` if you already have research and just want assets built. VSL, ads, emails, landing pages.
- `/outreach` for Dream 100 packages. Bespoke landing pages, deliverable hubs, email sequences for your prospects.
- `/niche` if you're still validating your niche.
- `/batch` if you want to run it for multiple clients back to back.

Start with `/pipeline`. Give it your business details. Let it run."
