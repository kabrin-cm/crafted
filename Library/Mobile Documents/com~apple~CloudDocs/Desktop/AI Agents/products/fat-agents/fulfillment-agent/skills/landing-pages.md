## Page Types & Structures

### Type 1: Sales Page (High Ticket / Application)

_Reference: Sell More Online Inner Circle, Client Ascension Explainer, Razza "Apply for The Formula"_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Outcome-driven headline (specific number or transformation)
   - Subheadline expanding on the promise
   - Video embed (VSL or explainer)
   - Primary CTA button (benefit-oriented, not "Sign Up")

2. **Problem Section**
   - Quantify the pain (use the pain math formula from direct-response-copy)
   - 3-5 specific pain points they recognize
   - "If this then that" scenario painting

3. **Solution Section**
   - What the offer is (framed as transformation, not features)
   - Unique mechanism — why THIS approach works differently
   - 3-4 key pillars or components of the solution

4. **Credibility Section**
   - Founder story (vulnerability → credibility → shared journey)
   - Revenue transparency or specific results
   - Authority logos, media mentions, or team profiles

5. **Social Proof Section**
   - 5-10 testimonials using the formula: [Before] + [Action] + [Specific Outcome] + [Timeframe] + [Emotion]
   - Mix of text and video testimonials
   - Highlight most impressive results first

6. **Offer Stack Section**
   - Everything included, with perceived value
   - Bonuses with individual value callouts
   - Total value vs. actual price (value anchoring)
   - Risk reversal (guarantee)

7. **Qualification Section**
   - "This is for you if..." (3-5 qualifying traits)
   - "This is NOT for you if..." (3-5 disqualifying traits)
   - Creates velvet rope effect

8. **FAQ Section**
   - Address top 5-7 objections
   - Use conversational tone, not corporate
   - Include the "If you could, you would have already" style responses where appropriate

9. **Final CTA Section**
   - Restate the transformation
   - Urgency (only if authentic — limited spots, deadline, etc.)
   - CTA button + friction reducers below: [Risk reversal] + [Social proof] + [Speed/ease]

### Type 2: Webinar/Masterclass Registration Page

_Reference: She Sells Remote Masterclass, Marshal Crews Q4 Blueprint_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - "FREE LIVE [MASTERCLASS/TRAINING/WORKSHOP]"
   - Headline: "How To [Specific Outcome] With [Host Name]" OR transformation headline ("From [bad state] to [good state] in [timeframe]")
   - Event date + time with timezone
   - Primary CTA: "SAVE MY FREE SPOT" / "REGISTER NOW" / "I'm In – Save My Seat"

2. **Urgency Bar (Optional but high-converting)**
   - Countdown timer: "GOING OFFLINE IN: 00:00:00:00"
   - "Seats are limited. Instant confirmation + reminders."
   - "Replay Only For Attendees" (replay scarcity)

3. **What You'll Learn Section**
   - "You're going to learn all this..."
   - 3-5 specific bullet points of what they'll discover
   - Each bullet = specific outcome, not vague promise
   - Include tangible deliverables: templates, checklists, scripts, calendars

4. **Qualification Section**
   - "This is for you if..." (aspirational traits)
   - "This is not for you if..." (disqualifying traits)
   - Audience targeting: "Beginners and brands earning $10K–$100K" (Marshal Crews)

5. **Host Bio Section**
   - "Hi, I'm [Name]" — personal, conversational
   - Credibility markers (results, experience, credentials)
   - Personal story connecting to the audience's struggle
   - Transformation story: "kicked out at 18 → multi-7-figure brand" (Marshal Crews)

6. **Final CTA Section**
   - "Register Your Spot Now"
   - Urgency: limited spots, specific date, replay uncertainty
   - Multiple CTA buttons throughout page (minimum 3)

### Type 3: Optin / Lead Magnet Page

_Reference: 7th Level Trial_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Headline: What they get + specific benefit
   - Subheadline: Who it's for + what it solves
   - Simple form (name + email minimum)
   - CTA: "Send Me [The Thing]" / "Get Instant Access"

2. **What's Inside Section**
   - 3-5 bullet points of what the lead magnet contains
   - Each bullet = specific outcome or piece of knowledge

3. **Credibility Mini-Section**
   - 1-2 sentences about who created it and why they're qualified
   - Optional: 1-2 short testimonials

4. **CTA Repeat**
   - Same form, same CTA
   - Friction reducer: "Free. No credit card required."

### Type 4: Waitlist / Coming Soon Page

_Reference: Monetise Waitlist_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Bold headline about what's coming
   - 1-2 sentences of intrigue (open loop)
   - Email capture: "Join the Waitlist"

2. **Tease Section (Optional)**
   - 2-3 things they'll get access to
   - Social proof: "Join X others already on the list"

3. **CTA**
   - Single email field + button
   - Scarcity: "Limited spots when we launch"

### Type 5: VSL Page

_Reference: Mastering VSLs SOP + Brand Scaling Bootcamp_

**Structure:**
1. **Curiosity Headline** (does NOT reveal the product)
2. **Video Player** (autoplay, no controls visible initially)
3. **CTA Button** (appears after key moment in video OR below fold)
4. **Minimal text below video** — the video does the selling
5. **Optional:** Testimonials below video, but page should be clean

### Type 6: Course/Blueprint Application Page

_Reference: Kayla Parrilla 7-Figure Blueprint, Brand Scaling (Brez)_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Time-bound headline: "If you're reading this in [month], You Could Have [outcome] by [date]"
   - Subheadline: "Learn the exact system [Name] used to [result] — and how you can duplicate it step-by-step"
   - CTA: "APPLY NOW - Use My [Blueprint Name]"

2. **Social Proof Counter**
   - "Join [X]+ students" with specific number (e.g. "227+")
   - Student count badge visible near hero

3. **What You'll Learn Section**
   - Specific modules or system components
   - Emphasis on "one product" or "one system" simplicity
   - Step-by-step framing

4. **Success Stories Section**
   - "Real Stories from Real Students"
   - Testimonial carousel with profile images
   - Specific outcomes (revenue, timeline, transformation)

5. **FAQ Section**
   - Objection handling
   - "How is this different from..." questions

6. **Final CTA**
   - Application-based (not direct purchase)
   - Repeat the time-bound urgency

### Type 7: Agency/Service Page

_Reference: Cobrax Creative, Infinia Growth, GMB Builder, Novify Media_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Industry-specific positioning headline: "[Superlative] [Service] in [Industry]"
   - Example: "Fastest Growing Marketing Company in Automotive" (Cobrax)
   - Subheadline with core value prop
   - CTA: "Book a Call" / "Get Started" / "Apply"

2. **Client Logo Bar**
   - 6-10 recognizable client/brand logos in a scrolling or grid row
   - Borrowed credibility — shows who trusts you

3. **Services/Features Grid**
   - Card-based layout with checkmarks or icons
   - 3-6 services/capabilities
   - Each card: icon + service name + 1-2 sentence description

4. **Case Studies / Results Section**
   - 2-4 specific client results with metrics
   - Before/after format or metric callouts
   - Optional: link to full case study

5. **Testimonials Section**
   - Client testimonials with company names and roles
   - Video testimonials if available

6. **Process Section (Optional)**
   - "How It Works" — 3-4 step process
   - Reduces perceived complexity

7. **CTA Section**
   - Calendar embed or application form
   - "Limited spots" or "Currently accepting X new clients"

### Type 8: Tiered Pricing / SaaS Page

_Reference: FanBasis PayVa, The Scale Club_

**Structure:**
1. **Hero Section**
   - Outcome-oriented headline: "Financing That Helps You Close More" (PayVa)
   - 3 key value props as bullet points or icons

2. **How It Works Section**
   - Simple 3-step process
   - Visual diagram or numbered steps

3. **Pricing Tier Table**
   - 2-4 tiers side by side (e.g. Pro / Prime / Premium)
   - Clear differentiation: what's included per tier
   - Highlight recommended tier
   - Transparent fees and terms

4. **Comparison Section**
   - "Us vs. Traditional [Alternative]" table
   - Side-by-side showing where you win
   - Example: "Traditional 30% total vs. PayVa 8%" (FanBasis)

5. **Eligibility/Requirements Section**
   - Who qualifies (functions as qualification + trust)
   - "Built for: [audience list]"
   - Requirements list shows you're selective

6. **FAQ + CTA**
   - Address pricing objections
   - Final CTA with tier selection

### Type 9: Event / Experience Page

_Reference: Caprice Entrepreneur Tour_

**Structure:**
1. **Atmospheric Hero** — immersive imagery/video, event name, "Join Us" CTA
2. **Event Details** — what, where, when, who's involved
3. **What You'll Experience** — highlight 3-5 key moments or benefits
4. **Speaker/Host Section** — bios with credibility
5. **Social Proof** — past event photos, attendee testimonials
6. **Registration CTA** — urgency (limited seats, specific dates)

## Design Guidelines

> For the full design system with hex codes, font stacks, and component specs, see `references/design-guide.md`

### Quick Reference

**Color by niche:**
- Trading/finance → Gold on dark (#eecb07, #d4af37)
- Marketing/agency → Purple on dark (#7803ff, #884bfa)
- E-commerce/brand → Red/neon on dark (#ff1f3d, #e31c25)
- SaaS/tech → Blue on dark (#0369cb, #2e54ff), Cyan (#32cae7)
- Female audience → Pink/magenta gradients (#fb96bc, #ff9ebb)
- Standout options → Lime green (#42CE09), Orange (#ff9e36)
- ~85% of high-converting pages use dark backgrounds

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Serif (Playfair Display, Instrument Serif) or geometric sans (Syne, Space Grotesk, Clash Display) — 48-72px desktop
- Body: Inter, DM Sans, or Plus Jakarta Sans — 16-18px, weight 300-500
- Inter is near-universal across the swipe file

**Layout:**
- Max-width 1200-1440px, centered
- 40-80px vertical spacing between sections
- Glassmorphic cards (semi-transparent, backdrop blur 5-8px) or bordered dark cards (border-radius 24-39px)
- Responsive breakpoints: 810px (tablet), 1200-1440px (desktop)

**CTAs:**
- Gradient backgrounds matching accent color
- Rounded corners (10-20px) or full pill (1000px border-radius)
- Benefit-oriented text, never generic
- Friction reducers below every CTA
- Optional animation (bounce/pulse) on primary CTA

## Copy Rules (Quick Reference)

From `direct-response-copy/SKILL.md` — always apply:

1. **Headlines:** [Action verb] + [Specific outcome] + [Timeframe or contrast]
2. **Opening:** Match their mental state, create curiosity, or make a bold claim
3. **Pain:** Quantify it. Do the math. Make it a number they can weigh against your price.
4. **Benefits:** Run the "So What?" chain 3 levels deep. Feature → Functional → Financial → Emotional.
5. **Testimonials:** [Before] + [Action] + [Specific Outcome] + [Timeframe] + [Emotion]
6. **CTAs:** Describe the benefit, not the action. Add friction reducers below.
7. **Disqualification:** Include "fit / not fit" section on sales pages
8. **Voice:** Internet-native, not corporate. Specific numbers, honest limitations, conversational tone.
9. **Flow:** Slippery slide — every element pulls them to the next. Bucket brigades, seeds of curiosity, vary paragraph length.
10. **AI tells:** Avoid overused words (delve, leverage, crucial, unlock). Read it out loud.

## Workflow

When asked to build a landing page:

1. **Identify the page type** (sales, webinar, optin, waitlist, VSL, course/blueprint, agency, pricing, event)
2. **Read the matching SOPs** from the SOP index
3. **Read the swipe file + design guide** for design and structural reference
4. **Gather inputs** from the user:
   - What's the offer?
   - Who's the audience?
   - What's the desired action? (apply, register, optin, buy)
   - What proof/testimonials exist?
   - What's the brand voice?
   - What niche? (determines color palette from design guide)
5. **Write the copy** section by section, following the structure for that page type
6. **Include design notes** with each section (color, typography, layout specs from the design guide)
7. **Deliver as:** Full page copy with section headings, CTA text, and design specs — ready to drop into Framer or any page builder

## Landing Page Considerations for Static Ad Traffic (SMO Enhancement)

When scaling with **static image ads** (as opposed to video ads), the landing page strategy should adapt:

**Key insight from SMO:** Static ads bring leads that have less intent and are overall less informed on you and your offer compared to video ad leads. Video ads pre-educate the prospect; static ads just get the click.

**Adjustments when traffic source is primarily statics:**
- Use a **more educational, longer-form landing page** with more copy, more case studies, and more trust elements
- **Weight VSL play rate and engagement rate more heavily** as a metric — the VSL has to do more heavy lifting since the ad didn't pre-educate
- Include additional proof sections that would normally be handled by the ad creative
- Consider adding inline breakout videos or expandable FAQ sections to increase time-on-page

**Static ads perform exceptionally well for:**
- Lead magnet funnels (the click is low-commitment)
- Webinar funnels (curiosity-driven, page does the selling)
- Challenge funnels (event-based urgency carries the conversion)

For standard VSL call funnels with static ad traffic, plan for a longer page with more educational content than you'd use with video ad traffic.
