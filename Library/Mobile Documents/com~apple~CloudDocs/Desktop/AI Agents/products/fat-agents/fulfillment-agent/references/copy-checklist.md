## 15. COPY QUALITY CHECKLIST

# Copywriting Quality Checklist
> **The 1-Page "Did I Apply Everything?" Reference**
>
> Use this before declaring any copy "done". If you can't check all boxes, you're not finished.

---

## HEADLINE (50-75% of effectiveness)

☐ **Self-interest formula?** Action verb + specific outcome + timeframe/contrast
☐ **Specificity?** Numbers, not generalities ("4,237" not "many")
☐ **Could be 19.5x better?** Tested at least 3 variations
☐ **Matches awareness level?** Unaware ≠ Most Aware headlines

**Quick test:** Remove your product name. Does headline still grab attention? If no → rewrite.

---

## OPENING (First 5 seconds)

☐ **First sentence makes them read second?** (Sugarman's slippery slide)
☐ **Uses proven pattern?** Story / Question / Challenge / Confession / Result
☐ **Zero AI giveaways?** No "In today's world..." / "Welcome!" / "Let's dive in"
☐ **Enters THEIR conversation?** Uses their words, not yours

**Quick test:** Read first 3 sentences aloud. Do you want to keep reading? If no → rewrite.

---

## MARKET INTELLIGENCE

☐ **Awareness level identified?** (1-Unaware → 5-Most Aware)
☐ **Message matches awareness?** Not skipping levels
☐ **Sophistication assessed?** (Stage 1-Pioneer → Stage 5-Identity)
☐ **Angle fits sophistication?** Stage 3+ needs mechanism, Stage 5 needs identity

**Quick test:** If market is saturated (Stage 3+), do you explain HOW it works? If no → add mechanism.

---

## PSYCHOLOGY (LF8 + Desires)

☐ **Primary LF8 clear?** Survival / Food / Pain-Free / Sex / Comfort / Superiority / Care / Social
☐ **Taps existing desire?** Not trying to create new wants
☐ **2-3 secondary LF8 woven in?** Reinforces from multiple angles

**Quick test:** What desire are you channeling? If you can't name it in 5 seconds → unclear focus.

---

## BODY COPY

☐ **Specific over vague?** ("$47,329" not "significant income")
☐ **Active voice?** ("We doubled revenue" not "Revenue was doubled")
☐ **One idea per paragraph?** No paragraph salad
☐ **Bucket brigades?** "But..." / "Here's why..." / "And..." / "Now..."
☐ **Open loops CLOSED?** Every curiosity gap resolved
☐ **Pattern interrupts?** Short sentence. After long ones. See?

**Quick test:** Read aloud. Do you stumble? If yes → simplify.

---

## PROOF & CREDIBILITY

☐ **Testimonials specific?** Names, numbers, results, timeframes
☐ **Proof near claims?** Don't separate "we're the best" from evidence
☐ **Authority transfer used?** "As seen in..." / "Trusted by..." / Expert quotes
☐ **Social proof quantified?** ("Join 50,000+ users" not "Join many")

**Quick test:** For each claim, is there proof within 2 paragraphs? If no → add or move proof.

---

## OBJECTIONS

☐ **Top 3 objections addressed?** Price / Time / Skepticism
☐ **Reframe patterns used?** Redefinition / Consequence / Proof / Reverse
☐ **Guarantee strong?** Risk reversal, specific terms
☐ **FAQ if needed?** Long-form copy needs objection section

**Quick test:** What would make YOU hesitate to buy? Is that objection handled? If no → add it.

---

## CALL TO ACTION

☐ **Formula applied?** Command verb + specific outcome + urgency
☐ **Friction words removed?** "Get" not "Purchase", "Start" not "Submit"
☐ **Repeated 3-5x?** (long copy only)
☐ **Clear next step?** No ambiguity about what happens when they click

**Quick test:** If you removed everything except the CTA, would they know what to do? If no → clarify.

---

## ADVANCED (NLP Patterns - if applicable)

☐ **Presuppositions embedded?** "When you experience results..." (assumes they will)
☐ **Verb tense shifting?** Problems = past, Solutions = present, Benefits = future
☐ **Submodality language?** "See yourself...", "Imagine...", sensory words
☐ **Embedded commands?** "You might want to *get started today*"

**Skip this section if you're not trained in NLP. These are advanced.**

---

## BANNED (AI Giveaways & Voice Killers)

☐ **ZERO em dashes (---)?** Use periods, commas, or line breaks
☐ **ZERO "no fluff" / "zero fluff"?** AI giveaway phrase
☐ **ZERO AI clichés?** No: leverage, utilize, unlock, empower, game-changer, dive deep, next-level, cutting-edge, revolutionary, secret sauce
☐ **NO 3+ short punchy sentences in a row?** AI cadence giveaway
☐ **NO "It's not X, it's Y" repetition?** One instance okay, pattern = AI

**If you find ANY of these → delete and rephrase immediately.**

---

## VOICE MATCH (Client Work Only)

☐ **Matches client voice profile?** Read theirs, analyzed patterns
☐ **Uses client's specific language?** Their words, not generic marketing
☐ **Tone appropriate?** Casual vs formal matches brand
☐ **Prices/dates/names correct?** Cross-referenced the business details you provide

**Quick test:** Could this copy be from ANY brand, or clearly from THIS client? If generic → personalize.

---

## FINAL QUALITY GATES

☐ **Read aloud?** Does it flow naturally?
☐ **Mobile-friendly?** Short paragraphs (2-4 lines max on mobile)
☐ **Scannability?** Subheads, bullets, bold for skim-readers
☐ **ONE clear next step?** Not 5 CTAs pointing different directions
☐ **Passed verify_copy.py?** (if available) Automated quality check

**Quick test:** Hand this to someone unfamiliar. Can they understand the offer and know what to do next in 30 seconds? If no → simplify structure.

---

## FORMAT-SPECIFIC

### If Landing Page:
☐ Awareness-appropriate structure
☐ CTA above fold + at end (minimum)
☐ Proof section exists

### If Email:
☐ Subject line = curiosity + self-interest
☐ One main idea only
☐ One CTA (repeated 2x max)

### If Ad:
☐ Hook in first line
☐ Problem agitation in 2-3 lines
☐ Social proof included
☐ Matches awareness of traffic source

### If VSL:
☐ Hook in first 60 seconds
☐ Problem → Credibility → Solution → Proof → Offer structure
☐ Pattern interrupts every 90 seconds
☐ CTA every 5 minutes after min 15

### If Sales Page (long-form):
☐ 7 Schwartz techniques applied
☐ Gradualization (string of yeses)
☐ Proof near all major claims
☐ P.S. restates benefit + deadline

---

## SCORING

**Count your checkmarks:**

- **45-50 ☐:** World-class copy. Ship it.
- **35-44 ☐:** Strong, but has gaps. Review unchecked items.
- **25-34 ☐:** Functional, not optimized. Needs work.
- **Below 25 ☐:** Incomplete. Not ready for review.

**Any BANNED item found = automatic fail. Fix before proceeding.**

---

## Emergency Fixes (If Copy Isn't Working)

**Low engagement? →** Check headline + opening (first 5 seconds)
**Low conversions? →** Check objection handling + CTA clarity
**Sounds like AI? →** Check banned phrases + voice match
**Too long? →** Every paragraph should pull to next. Cut what doesn't.
**Too short? →** Add proof, mechanism explanation, objection handling

---

## The 3 Non-Negotiables

If you do nothing else, do these:

1. **Specificity over vague.** Always.
2. **Match awareness level.** Don't skip stages.
3. **Zero AI giveaways.** Read banned list. Delete on sight.
