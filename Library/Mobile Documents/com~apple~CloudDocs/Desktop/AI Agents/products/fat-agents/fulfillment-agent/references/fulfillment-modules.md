## When This Module Activates
Client needs VSL scripts, sales pages, or sales letters for their offer or their clients' offers.

## Capabilities

1. Pattern Interrupt (0:00-0:30)
2. Core Problem (0:30-1:30)
3. Agitate (1:30-2:30)
4. Credential Proof (2:30-3:30)
5. Unique Mechanism (3:30-5:00)
6. Future Pace (5:00-6:00)
7. Offer Stack (6:00-8:00)
8. Risk Reversal (8:00-8:30)
9. CTA (8:30-9:00)

### Email Sequences

# Module: Email Sequences

## When This Module Activates
Client needs email automation: welcome sequences, nurture flows, pre-call, post-call, no-show, launch sequences, or broadcast emails.

## Capabilities
- Pre-call sequences (7 emails / 72 hours) for show-rate optimization
- Post-call sequences (5 emails / 7 days) for no-close follow-up
- No-show sequences (3 emails / 48 hours) for rebooking
- Welcome / post-purchase sequences (5 emails / 14 days) for retention
- Nurture / broadcast emails (ongoing value + pitch ratio)
- Launch sequences (pre-launch, cart open, cart close)
- Re-engagement sequences (win-back for cold subscribers)
- Subject lines + preview text for every email
- Platform-specific formatting (ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign, GoHighLevel, Kit)

## Key Principles
- One clear CTA per email
- Under 250 words per email (under 150 for cold outreach)
- Value-first ratio: 3 value emails per 1 pitch email
- Each email can standalone (reader may not see previous)
- Subject lines are curiosity-driven and specific
- P.S. lines for urgency or proof

### Content & Social

# Module: Content & Social Media

## When This Module Activates
Client needs social media content, blog posts, content strategy, or content atomization.

## Capabilities
- Social media posts (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook, TikTok)
- Blog articles and SEO content
- Newsletter writing
- Short-form content (hooks, captions, one-liners)
- Content atomization (one piece into 13+ platform-native assets)
- Content calendar planning
- Hashtag and engagement strategy

## Content Atomization Framework
Given one long-form piece (podcast, video, article):
1. Extract 5-10 standalone insights
2. Create platform-native versions:
   - LinkedIn: 150-300 word story-driven post
   - Instagram: Carousel (5-7 slides) or caption (100-150 words)
   - X/Twitter: Thread (5-8 tweets) or single tweet
   - YouTube Shorts/Reels: 30-60 second script
   - Blog: 800-1500 word article
   - Newsletter: 500-800 word email
3. Each piece gets its own unique hook
4. Maintain original voice and specifics

## Platform-Specific Rules
- **LinkedIn**: Story format, professional tone, no hashtag spam (3-5 max), strong opening line
- **Instagram**: Visual-first, shorter copy, carousel for education, Reels for reach
- **X/Twitter**: Threads for depth, singles for engagement, controversal hooks work
- **TikTok**: Script-first (what they say matters more than production), trending sounds

### Landing Pages

# Module: Landing Pages & Funnels

## When This Module Activates
Client needs landing page copy, opt-in pages, webinar registration pages, confirmation pages, or complete funnel builds.

## Capabilities
- Sales page copy (long-form, 10+ sections)
- Opt-in page copy (lead magnet delivery)
- Webinar registration pages
- Challenge registration pages
- Confirmation/thank-you pages
- Post-booking pages
- Lead magnet design and copy
- Trust asset creation (case studies, testimonials)

## Landing Page Structure (10+ Sections)
1. Hero (headline + subheadline + CTA + VSL embed)
2. Problem (3-5 specific pain points)
3. Solution (introduce the mechanism)
4. How It Works (3-4 step process)
5. Proof (testimonials, case studies, screenshots)
6. Offer Stack (everything included, value anchored)
7. Bonus Section (time-sensitive bonuses)
8. Guarantee (risk reversal)
9. FAQ (5-7 objections as questions)
10. Final CTA (restate transformation + urgency)

## Headline Formulas
- [Number] [Audience] [Achieved Result] [Without Common Objection]
- How to [Desired Outcome] in [Timeframe] [Without Pain Point]
- The [Mechanism Name] That [Specific Result] for [Audience]
- [Audience]: Stop [Common Mistake]. Start [Better Approach].
