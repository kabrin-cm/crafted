## 1. BRAND VOICE ANALYSIS

### Voice Attributes
- **Tone:** Empowering, supportive, no-nonsense
- **Personality:** Mom-to-mom relatable, professional but not clinical
- **Approach:** Anti-diet, sustainable-first, evidence-based
- **Key Value:** "Not another diet — it's a lifestyle"

### Use These Phrases
- "Lifestyle method"
- "Sustainable results"
- "Hormone optimization"
- "Food neutrality"
- "Best shape of your life"
- "Without giving up the foods you love"

### Avoid
- "Diet" (use "lifestyle" instead)
- "Quick fix"

## 2. IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)

### Demographics
- **Age:** 25-45
- **Gender:** Women (primarily moms)
- **Income:** $50K+ disposable
- **Location:** English-speaking (US/Canada/UK/Australia)

### Psychographics
- Frustrated dieter — tried keto, IF, meal plans, nothing worked
- Time-starved — juggling work, kids, responsibilities
- Wants sustainability — tired of yoyo weight loss
- Values education — wants to understand WHY
- Skeptical — been burned by quick fixes

### Pain Points
1. Feeling deprived on every diet
2. Weight loss that doesn't last
3. Confusion about what actually works
4. No personalized guidance
5. Can't give up favorite foods
6. Low energy / poor sleep

### Desired Outcomes
- Lose weight WITHOUT feeling deprived
- More energy
- Better sleep
- Sustainable habits
- Confidence

## 4. OFFER ANALYSIS (Daniel Fazio + Alex Hormozi)

### Current Offer

| Component | Current State | Opportunity |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Claim** | "Best shape of your life" | Add specific: "lose 10-20lbs in 90 days" |
| **Guarantee** | None | Add guarantee? |
| **Evidence** | 2 case studies (Kaitlin, Rianne) | Add more, video testimonials |
| **USP** | Hormone focus + NOT meal plans | Lean harder into anti-diet |

### Hormozi Value Equation

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort)**

- **Dream Outcome:** Best shape of their life
- **Likelihood:** Case studies increase belief
- **Time Delay:** Emphasize "sustainable" vs quick
- **Effort:** "Not meal plans" = less effort

### Recommendations
1. **Strengthen Claim:** Add specific numbers
2. **Add Guarantee:** "Results that last or refund"
3. **Enhance Evidence:** More testimonials, video
4. **Deepen USP:** "We teach HOW to eat, not WHAT"
