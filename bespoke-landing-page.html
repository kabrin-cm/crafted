Common Questions

What about securities regulations for marketing exempt market products?
All ad copy and landing pages would go through your compliance review before launch. We build within regulatory frameworks. The lead magnet educates about MIC investing generally, not making specific return promises in ads.
How quickly would we see results?
First leads typically come within the first week of launching ads. Meaningful data for optimization takes 4-6 weeks. Full pipeline maturity (ads + email nurture + consultations) is typically 60-90 days.
What if our competitors start running ads too?
First-mover advantage compounds. By the time a competitor launches, you'll have months of pixel data, optimized campaigns, and a growing email list. They'll be paying more for the same leads you're already getting cheaply.
What's the minimum ad budget?
We recommend starting at $4,500/month across Meta and Google. That gives enough volume for meaningful testing and optimization. Scale up as results prove out.
Do we need to change our website?
No. Paid campaigns drive to dedicated landing pages, not your existing website. The landing pages are purpose-built for conversion. Your website stays as-is.
