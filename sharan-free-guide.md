## Part 1: Why Diets Always Fail

### The Diet Trap

Diets work... for about 3-6 weeks.

Then something happens. You hit a plateau. You get tired of restriction. You "cheat" once and suddenly you're back to where you started.

**Why does this happen?**

Because diets are Band-Aids. They don't teach you anything. They just tell you what to eat for a short period of time.

Once you stop following the diet? You go back to normal.

And "normal" is what got you here in the first place.

### The Problem With Willpower

"Just have more willpower."

"If you really wanted it, you'd stick to the plan."

"You've got to be disciplined."

**Sound familiar?**

Here's the thing: Willpower is a finite resource. You can't run on willpower forever.

That's why diets fail. They rely on willpower. And eventually, willpower runs out.

### The Real Problem: Your Hormones

Here's what nobody talks about:

**Your hormones control everything.**

- Cortisol (stress hormone) makes you store belly fat
- Insulin determines whether you burn fat or store it
- Leptin tells your brain when you're full
- Thyroid controls your metabolism

When your hormones are out of balance? No diet in the world will help.

That's why you've been stuck.

## Part 3: The 3-Day Reset

Here's a simple starting point. Try this for 3 days:

### Day 1: No Scale

**Rule:** Don't weigh yourself.

**Why:** Daily weight fluctuations are normal and caused by water retention, sodium, hormones, and more. Weighing yourself daily creates emotional rollercoasters.

**Action:** Focus on how you FEEL instead of what you weigh.

### Day 2: Add Protein + Vegetables

**Rule:** Every meal should have protein and vegetables.

**Why:** Protein keeps you full longer. Vegetables provide nutrients that support hormone balance.

**Action:** For breakfast, add eggs or Greek yogurt. For lunch/dinner, fill half your plate with vegetables.

### Day 3: Movement You Enjoy

**Rule:** Move your body in a way you actually like.

**Why:** Exercise shouldn't be punishment. And forced exercise you hate = won't stick.

**Action:** Walk, dance, swim, yoga, weights... whatever you enjoy. Do it for 20-30 minutes.
