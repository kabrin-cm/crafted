Frequently Asked Questions

How are my returns generated?
Metropointe MIC lends pooled investor capital as mortgages secured by BC real estate. Borrowers pay interest on those mortgages. Your share of the interest earned is distributed to you as quarterly dividends.
What secures my investment?
Every mortgage is secured by registered title on BC real property. In the event of a borrower default, the property is sold to recover the capital. The portfolio is diversified across multiple properties and borrowers to reduce concentration risk.
Can I hold MIC shares in my RRSP or TFSA?
Yes. MIC shares are eligible for RRSP, TFSA, RRIF, and RESP accounts. Returns earned inside registered accounts are completely tax-sheltered.
What's the minimum investment?
Contact Metropointe Capital for current minimum investment requirements. Both personal and corporate investment options are available.
How do I access my money if I need it?
MIC shares have defined redemption periods. This structure protects all investors from forced liquidation during market downturns and allows the fund to maintain stable returns.
Who manages the fund?
Metropointe MIC was co-founded and is directed by Suki Gill, CPA, CGA. The fund has been managed with consistent returns since 2011. Metropointe Capital Inc. operates as the Exempt Market Dealer, regulated by BCFSA.
What if BC real estate prices decline?
Mortgages are underwritten at conservative loan-to-value ratios, providing an equity cushion. Even in a correction, the security buffer protects investor capital. The portfolio's diversification across property types and locations provides additional protection.
